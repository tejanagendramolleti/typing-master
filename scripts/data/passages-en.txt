@@META: EN|GENERAL|HARD
@@CONTENT:
The market committee has resolved to amend the timings of the wholesale vegetable market with effect from the beginning of the next season. The gates will be opened at four in the morning instead of five, and the auction of the vegetables arriving by the night road will be held from half past four. The traders who bring their goods by the morning road will continue to have the auction at six, and the committee has decided that the two auctions will not overlap so that the weighment of the first lot is not disturbed. The committee has also decided that the second weighbridge, which has been out of order, will be put in use again, as the delay in the weighment has been the cause of most of the complaints. The storage sheds will be allotted on the basis of the payment of the rent for the previous season, and the pending cases will be taken up on the day of the allotment. The committee has asked the traders to bring the goods in clean crates, and the ones that do not comply will be charged a higher rate for the carriage.

@@TITLE: Guidelines for the Revival of the Dormant Cooperative Societies
@@META: EN|GENERAL|HARD
@@CONTENT:
The department has issued guidelines for the revival of the cooperative societies that have been inactive for a period of more than three years, as a result of which the office has found it difficult to complete the audit of the accounts and to settle the outstanding claims. The office will prepare a list of the dormant societies with the assistance of the district cooperative officer, and the list will be published for the information of the members. The revival of a society will be taken up on the application of not less than one third of the members, and the application must be accompanied by the reasons for the earlier inactivity and by a plan for the recovery of the dues. A committee of the office will examine the application and will recommend a course of action within sixty days. The society that is revived will be given a period of two years to clear the dues, and the office will examine the progress of the recovery at the end of each year. A society that fails to show progress will be wound up under the provisions of the Act.

@@TITLE: Circular on the Revision of the Staffing Norms for the Health Centres
@@META: EN|GENERAL|HARD
@@CONTENT:
The department has revised the staffing norms for the health centres with effect from the first day of the next financial year. The sanction of the staff for a health centre will now depend on the population served and on the average number of the daily attendance in the previous year, and both the figures will be recorded in the application for the sanction. A centre that has an attendance of more than the norm prescribed for its category will be given an additional post of a medical officer, and the additional post will be given on a contract basis for a period of two years at the first. The centres that have a very low attendance will be examined for a merger with a neighbouring centre, and the decision on the merger will be taken by the district health officer after a survey of the area. The revision is expected to correct the imbalance in the distribution of the staff, which was the finding of the study commissioned by the government. The norms will be reviewed after three years, and the data of the intervening period will be used for the review.

@@TITLE: Notice on the Change in the Rules for the Grant of the Building Permission
@@META: EN|GENERAL|HARD
@@CONTENT:
The office of the commissioner has issued a notice amending the rules for the grant of the building permission in the municipal area. The application for the permission will now have to be accompanied by the certificate of the structural design of the building, drawn up by an engineer who is registered with the department, and the certificate of the plan of the building that is drawn up by an architect who is registered with the council of the profession. The setback from the road and the open space required for the building will be calculated from the record of the layout of the town, and the open space will have to be of the size given in the annexure to the rules. The permission will lapse if the construction is not begun within six months from the date of the grant.

@@TITLE: Guidelines for the Documentation of the Oral History
@@META: EN|GENERAL|HARD
@@CONTENT:
The state archives have issued guidelines for the documentation of the oral history of the older persons of the district, which is a part of the programme for the collection of the material that is not available in written form. A person who wishes to record the recollections of a resident must first obtain the consent of the person concerned in writing, and the consent must be recorded in the form provided for the purpose. The recording will be made in the language in which the person is comfortable, and the interpreter will translate it at a later stage. The recording must cover the account of the family, the work, the festivals and the events that the person witnessed, and the interviewer must ask the questions that are given in the schedule so that the accounts of different persons can be compared. The recording must be kept for a period of ten years before it is opened for the research, and the person who gave the account may ask for it to be kept closed for a longer period. A copy of the recording will be given to the family of the person and a copy of the transcript will be deposited with the district library.

@@TITLE: Circular on the Standard of the Advertisement for the Public Schemes
@@META: EN|GENERAL|HARD
@@CONTENT:
The department has issued a circular prescribing the standard that the advertisement for the public schemes of the government must follow. The advertisement must be in the language of the district and must give the eligibility of the scheme in plain words, without the use of the technical expression that the applicant may not understand. The figures of the benefit must be shown as they are given in the sanction order, and no amount may be shown that has not been approved. The advertisement must carry the name of the office where the application is to be made and the last date on which the application will be received, and these two particulars must appear in the same size as the rest of the text. The photograph of a person used in the advertisement must be that of a real beneficiary, and the consent for the use of the photograph must be obtained in writing. The department will approve the advertisement before it is released, and no advertisement may be displayed without that approval.

@@CONTENT:
The civil supplies department has issued a notice on the verification of the ration cards of the households in the district. The ration card that has not been verified for two consecutive years will be suspended, and the household will have to apply afresh for the card, which will take the time that is given in the rules. @@TITLE: Guidelines for the Handling of the Grievances in the Public Grievance Cell
@@META: EN|GENERAL|HARD
@@CONTENT:
The department has issued guidelines for the handling of the grievances that are received in the public grievance cell of the district. Every grievance must be recorded in the software with the date and the time of its receipt, and the person who brings the grievance must be given a receipt with the number of the grievance. The grievances will be classified as immediate, which are those that concern the supply of water, the power or the medical care, and as ordinary, which are the rest. An immediate grievance must be attended to within twenty-four hours and the person must be told what has been done within three days. An ordinary grievance will be forwarded to the office that is competent to deal with it, with a copy of the record, and the reply must be received within thirty days. A person who is not satisfied with the reply may appeal to the designated officer, and the appeal will be decided within fifteen days. The cell will publish a monthly summary of the grievances received, the nature of the grievances and the time taken for the disposal.

@@TITLE: Notice on the Protection of the Trees in the Public Land
@@META: EN|GENERAL|HARD
@@CONTENT:
The municipal office has issued a notice on the protection of the trees that stand on the public land and on the land under the control of the office, following the damage that was noticed to a number of trees along the main avenue. No person shall cut, lop, uproot or damage any tree on the public land, or cause the damage by any other means, and the offence will attract the penalty that the rules provide. The office will carry out the removal of the dead and the fallen branches, and the work will be taken up after the flowering season so that the birds are not disturbed. The office will plant a new tree for every tree that is lost, and the species will be chosen by the office having regard to the nature of the soil.

@@TITLE: Guidelines for the Assessment of the Work of the Field Staff
@@META: EN|GENERAL|HARD
@@CONTENT:
The department has issued guidelines for the assessment of the work of the field staff who are attached to the offices of the subdivision. The assessment will be made against the work plan that is approved for the year, and not against the number of the visits made, which is the practice at present. The work plan will state the number of the households to be covered, the number of the cases to be settled and the number of the schemes to be verified, together with the standard that is expected for each of them. A member of the field staff whose work is found to be unsatisfactory will be given an opportunity to explain before any adverse entry is made in the record. The record of the assessment will count towards the promotion of the staff, and three years of the record will be considered for the purpose.
